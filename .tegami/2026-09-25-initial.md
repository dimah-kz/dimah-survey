### Publish the SurveyJS response lifecycle

`@dimah-survey/server` owns publish, the per-response definition snapshot, drafts, and submit validation with survey-core. `@dimah-survey/core` is the protocol and fetch client. `@dimah-survey/react` binds a survey-core Model. `@dimah-survey/db` is the SQL store.
