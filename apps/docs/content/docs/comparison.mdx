Use dimah-survey when SurveyJS is the form and you want publishing and
responses in your own backend, with the same kind of integration as
[Better Auth](https://www.better-auth.com).

<Callout>
  SurveyJS stays the schema and the UI. dimah-survey is the server around them.
</Callout>

## A good fit

- surveys should live in your database, behind your auth
- authors edit a draft and publish when respondents should see it
- save, submit, abandon, and reopen go through one API
- a response should still match the survey the person filled, after you publish again
- you want to keep ownership of auth, data, and deployment

## Skip it when

- you only need to render a SurveyJS form
- you want a hosted survey product
- you are not using SurveyJS JSON

## Ownership

| Concern                     | dimah-survey           | SurveyJS alone          | Hosted product   |
| --------------------------- | ---------------------- | ----------------------- | ---------------- |
| Survey renderer             | Your app uses SurveyJS | Your app uses SurveyJS  | Product or embed |
| Publish                     | Built in               | Your implementation     | Product workflow |
| Survey stored on a response | Built in               | Your persistence design | Vendor-specific  |
| Draft, submit, and reopen   | Built in               | Your implementation     | Product workflow |
| Authentication              | Your application       | Your application        | Vendor account   |
| Data and migrations         | Your application       | Your application        | Vendor storage   |
| Authoring                   | Creator in your app    | Survey Creator          | Hosted builder   |

This compares ownership, not performance.

## Outside this library

dimah-survey does not render questions, ship Creator, store uploaded files, or
provide accounts, dashboards, PDF export, or analytics.

SurveyJS Analytics or PDF tooling can use one published document together with
the `data` rows that point at it. Read older answers with the survey stored on
that response.
