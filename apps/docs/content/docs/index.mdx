dimah-survey is the backend for [SurveyJS](https://surveyjs.io/) surveys. It
stores each survey, publishes it, and collects responses over HTTP. SurveyJS
draws the form. Your application keeps authentication, the database, and the
UI.

The architecture matches [Better Auth](https://www.better-auth.com): create an
instance, pass your database, and mount the handler.

Mount two handlers on one store. They do not share routes.

| Audience | Caller           | Access                                                     |
| -------- | ---------------- | ---------------------------------------------------------- |
| Fill     | Respondents      | Read a published survey and work on their own response     |
| Editor   | Your application | Edit the draft, publish, change settings, and read results |

## What the server does

- Saves the editor draft, and publishes only when you ask
- Stores progress while someone is answering, then checks the submission
- Applies collection rules: who may answer again, when the survey is open, and how many submissions it accepts
- Runs on Next.js, Hono, Express, Fastify, Elysia, SvelteKit, and Node

A response keeps the published survey it started with. A later edit or publish
does not change that copy, and submit is checked against it. See
[Draft and publish](/docs/surveys) and [Responses](/docs/responses).
